# Our little hero’s handbook

**One hero. Two costumes. Your whole family to rescue!**

1. Click **Let’s play**. Move with the **arrow keys** (or W / A / D plus Down Arrow).
2. Press **Space** to jump. Follow the floating sign.
3. Press **Q** to change form. You stay in the same place!
4. **Form A** runs faster. Press **K** to dash through arches.
5. **Form B** jumps higher. Press **K** to pop cracked crystals.
6. Press **E** beside a glowing item to pick it up. E also uses numbered machines and portals.
7. Press **J** to give a shadow puppet a friendly cartoon bop.

When a big shadow warns you, move away. When its ring becomes **pale green**, it is resting: your turn to attack!

The rest stone refills your hearts. Finished jobs save by themselves. A little tumble is okay: **R** tries again, and your completed jobs are safe.

After a rescue, press **Tab** to choose a family helper. Press **L** to call them. They stay beside you for **five seconds**! The little bar shows when they can help again. You can also click **Choose** to read their powers.

Story scenes play by themselves. **Read at my pace** lets you turn each page with **E** or **Space**. You can click **Skip scene** too.

**Escape** pauses for a snack. Settings has **Gentle adventure**, sound and fewer effects. The game is fun with sound off.

Click **Full screen** at the top of the game, or choose it in **Settings**, to fill your screen. Click **Exit full screen** or press **Escape** to return to the window. Press Escape again to pause.

**Lost?** Follow the floating sign and its distance. **Map** takes you back to the worlds. **Pause → Restart this objective** lets you try the current job again; your earlier jobs stay done.

## Playing with a PS5 controller

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Left stick | Move |
| × (Cross) | Jump / fly up |
| ○ (Circle) | Special ability, including activating Hyper Goku |
| □ (Square) | Attack |
| △ (Triangle) | Regular hero: change form. Goku / Hyper Goku: fly down. LeBron / Curry / Vini: regular shot. |
| Options | Pause / resume |
| L3 + R3 together | Super Goku / normal form |
| R3 alone | Interact / next scene |
| L2 | Choose the next ally |
| R2 | Summon the selected ally |
| R1 | Equip / unequip LeBron James, when unlocked |
| L1 | Equip / unequip Steph Curry, when unlocked |
| D-pad Up | Equip / unequip Vini Jr., when unlocked |
| D-pad Down as Vini | Celebrate |

**L3 / R3 mean clicking the sticks down.** Click both together to transform; a solo R3 click interacts when released, so the two actions never fire together. The transformation requires all seven Chaos Emeralds.

In menus, move either joystick or use the D-pad to choose a button, press **×** to select, and **○** to go back. Left / right adjusts a selected setting. Circle does nothing on the main menu itself.

To change these buttons, open **Settings → Remap controller buttons**. Pick each button from its list, then choose **Save buttons**. The layout applies to both players and stays saved in this browser. **Reset defaults** restores the layout above; **Cancel** discards unsaved changes. Triangle can share different actions because only one character action applies at a time.

## Bring a teammate!

Pick **Two players** in Settings, then enter or retry a world. The first connected controller controls Player one; the second controls Player two. You can also share a keyboard: P1 uses W/A/D/X, Space, J, K and Q; P2 uses arrows, 0, 1, 2 and 3. P2 uses Enter to interact, M to choose an ally and N to summon.

Your missions are shared. Friendly fire is on by default, and player attacks bypass all invincibility. Turn it off in Settings whenever you like. Defeated players respawn after **3 seconds**. Arrows above the heroes identify Player one and Player two. Both players can wear the same unlocked character; use **Characters** in the HUD or pause menu to choose.

The adventure is designed for about **30 minutes** the first time. Take your time — there is no race, and you can save it for later.

## Chaos Emeralds and Super Goku

After finishing the main story, collect **all 158 stars** across all seven stages, including the Origin and finale. The map shows each stage's star count. Then open **Chaos Emeralds** on the map and complete the seven side missions.

The Chaos Emeralds are **green, purple, silver, blue, cyan, yellow and red**. Open three glowing seals in each mission, then walk into its emerald. Your seals and emeralds save automatically.

Once you have all seven:

- Press **S** or click **Super**. The emeralds circle the screen, spiral into you, and turn you into **Super Goku**!
- Hold **Space** to fly up. Release it to hover. Hold **C** to fly down. Use the **arrow keys** to steer.
- Press **S** again. The emeralds shoot out across the screen and you return to your original form, with normal gravity.
- Press **S** whenever you want to transform again. You keep every emerald!

On a controller, **click both sticks (L3 + R3)** to transform or return, **×** rises and **△** descends. In two-player mode each player can transform separately. Touch players can use **Super**, the jump button to rise, and **Fly down** to descend.

**S is now Super. Use Down Arrow to move backwards.**


## Hyper power and replays

As Super Goku, **K / ○** activates Hyper Goku for **15 seconds**. **J / □** fires the giant blue beam. Hyper recharges for **5 seconds** after ending. Both Goku forms ignore enemy damage; player attacks still count when friendly fire is on.

Choose **Replay a level** from the menu or map, then pick a completed level, **One player / Two players**, and an unlocked character. Both heroes use that starting character; you can change them separately while playing. Quests, enemies and bosses return while your real story progress and collected stars stay safe. Newly found stars and secret characters still save.

Secret characters explain their controls when you discover them; the handbook then keeps those controls available. Both players may use any discovered character. Controller users can select characters through the pause menu. **△** shoots a regular sports ball, **○** uses the special shot, **R1** toggles LeBron, **L1** toggles Curry, and **D-pad Up** toggles Vini. **D-pad Down** celebrates while playing as Vini.

## Shadow Mayhem

Choose **World map → Shadow Mayhem** after beating the game and unlocking all characters: both hero forms, Super Goku/Hyper Goku, LeBron James, Steph Curry, Vini Jr. and Handsome Squidward, with all five allies rescued.

- 5,000 puppets start the run, then up to 5,000 arrive every 30 seconds.
- At most 500,000 ordinary puppets roam at once; defeating them makes room at the next wave.
- 50,000 Ultimate Shadow Bosses arrive every 60 seconds, separately from the puppet cap.
- No time limit, final wave or total defeat limit. The HUD counts puppet and boss defeats.
- Choose one or two players; both fight the shadows, and the Settings PvP toggle also enables attacks and family abilities against each other. Defeated players respawn after three seconds.
- Pause freezes the wave timers. Use Map to leave; a new visit starts a fresh run. Your story checkpoint stays saved.

Triangle / T now shoots a regular basketball or football as LeBron, Curry or Vini in any world. Near a stadium hoop or goal it aims for a score; elsewhere it fires forwards and can hit shadows. Circle / K uses the flaming shot.

Controller menus: use either stick or the D-pad to move; hold to scroll. X selects. On a dropdown or slider, X enters adjustment, directions change the value, and X or Circle finishes. Circle goes back; Options pauses/resumes. All story buttons, header buttons, level/replay/Mayhem pickers, settings, characters and allies are reachable. When the browser blocks controller-triggered native fullscreen, the game expands to fill the window.

At extreme Mayhem populations the game batches enemy AI and draws a representative crowd. Every spawned enemy still has its own position and health; damage and defeat counting include the full population.


### Handsome Squidward — Ultimate Rizzler

Shoot Hyper Goku’s beam at a round wall mirror beside the drawers in the Palace of Perfection. The unlock message confirms the skin is permanently saved. D-pad Left equips or unequips it for either player; keyboard H for P1, O for P2. It is also in Characters and replay selection. This cosmetic skin can walk, jump and interact, but cannot attack, summon allies or use powers.

Mummy’s potion overlays Handsome Squidward on nearby enemies for eight seconds, including bosses and the Mayhem crowd. With PvP on, Mummy transforms the opposing player for four seconds, Nana prevents attacks for three seconds, Grandad throws them, Leah damages them, and Daddy’s football can hit them. These family attacks do not affect the other player when PvP is off.
