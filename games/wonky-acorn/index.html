Wonky Acorn supports PS5, PS4, Xbox, and Switch Pro controllers via USB or Bluetooth.

PlayStation 5 / 4

  1. USB-C / Micro-USB cable into your computer
  2. OR Bluetooth: hold PS + Share for 3 seconds — light bar flashes blue
  3. On your computer: System Settings → Bluetooth → connect "Wireless Controller"

Xbox Series / One

  1. USB cable into your computer
  2. OR Bluetooth: hold the small Pair button (top of controller) for 3 seconds
  3. System Settings → Bluetooth → connect "Xbox Wireless Controller"

Switch Pro Controller

  1. USB-C cable into your computer
  2. OR Bluetooth: hold the Sync button (top of controller, next to USB port) for 3 seconds
  3. System Settings → Bluetooth → connect "Pro Controller"

Test it

Open this page in Chrome/Edge and press a button on the controller. The HUD should react. If nothing happens, refresh the page after pairing.
